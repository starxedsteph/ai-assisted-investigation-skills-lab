# Lab data

## Amazon reviews

`fake-reviews-data.csv` contains the first 10,000 records from
[Brett Hollenbeck's March 2026 export](https://github.com/bretthollenbeck/fake-reviews-data),
not a random sample. It includes product and reviewer IDs, review titles and
text, ratings, and dates. Campaign labels and removal annotations are excluded;
formatting controls and spreadsheet-like prefixes have been safety-transformed.

The sample is shared under the [MIT license](LICENSE-fake-reviews-data.txt).
